About

A focused practice, built on responsiveness.

Based inGoregaon West, Mumbai
Practising since2023 (3 years)
Core areasCivil & Property
Client styleDirect, personal

Property and civil matters move slowly when no one is pushing them forward. My practice is built to do exactly that — clear communication, realistic timelines, and a lawyer who actually picks up the phone.

I handle everything from title verification before a purchase, to long-running partition and tenancy disputes, to drafting the agreements that prevent disputes in the first place. Every matter is reviewed personally; nothing is handed off and forgotten.

Being early in practice means one thing to clients: I'm building my name on getting your matter right, not coasting on a reputation I already have. That shows up in how closely I stay on a file.
